JOSH is a creator-first video platform.

Watch, upload and grow. Creators keep 60% of recorded revenue, the platform keeps 40% — calculated in integer micro-USDT so rounding never invents or loses a unit.

Upload a video Open Creator Studio

Real numbers only

Views are counted when playback actually starts, once per viewer per video. Likes, comments and subscribers are stored records. There are no placeholder videos and no estimated earnings anywhere in the interface.

Creator Studio

Every upload with its genuine views, likes and comments, plus a 14-day view chart built from real view timestamps. Drafts stay private until you publish them.

Earnings and payouts

A transparent 60/40 split, a revenue ledger that only shows recorded payments, and USDT BEP20 withdrawal requests from 0.5 USDT with administrator review.

Honest integrations

Payments are not connected, so the platform states that plainly instead of implying money is moving. Livestreaming shows a setup state until a provider is configured.

Prepared for Supabase

The database schema, row-level security policies and storage rules for permanent accounts and video uploads are included in supabase/schema.sql. Until Supabase is connected, accounts and files live in this deployment's own store.
